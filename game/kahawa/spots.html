Kahawa scene maker

Add a new drawing to Kahawa Iko Wapi? by marking the places a coffee bag could hide.

  1. Pick a black-and-white line drawing (PNG, JPG, WEBP or SVG, portrait works best, about 1200 px wide or more).
  2. Click the drawing wherever a bag could sit: on a roof, a shelf, a cloud, in a bush. Drag to move, use the sliders to size and tilt. Mark at least 30: the game uses 12 a day and picks different ones each time.
  3. Download the scene file. Put the picture in game/kahawa/scenes/ and the .json in server/kahawa/scenes/, then restart the server.
