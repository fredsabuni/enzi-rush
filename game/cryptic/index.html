How cryptic clues work

Sixty seconds, three clues
  1. Every clue has two parts: a definition (a plain meaning of the answer) at the start or end, and wordplay that builds the same answer another way.
  2. Common tricks: anagrams (“unusual”, “broken”, “wild”), hidden words (“in”, “part of”, “some”), reversals (“back”, “returned”), initials (“initially”, “at first”) and charades (A + B side by side).
  3. The number in brackets is the letter count. (3,5) means two words.

How to play

Scores are worked out when you finish
  1. Tap a square to pick a word. Tap it again to switch between Across and Down.
  2. Type to fill in letters. ⌫ deletes. ‹ › in the clue bar move between clues. On a keyboard: arrows move, Tab jumps to the next clue.
  3. Hints help when you're stuck: check a letter or word, show which part of the clue is the definition, explain the clue, or reveal letters. Each one costs points.
  4. Fill every square correctly to finish. Faster solves and fewer hints score more; no hints at all earns a 20% bonus.
  5. The Quick is new every day at 05:00. The Weekly Classic is new every Monday, and the timer only runs while you're playing, so you can come back to it on another visit, even on another phone.
